Advancing a Stage

Open a client's case → scroll to the Milestones section → click Advance Stage once the current stage requirements are confirmed complete. This triggers an automatic client notification and updates the portal dashboard.

Rule: Never advance a stage unless documents for that stage are verified and any associated payment is confirmed. Advancing a stage is a formal record — it cannot be reversed without contacting the system administrator.

Putting a Case On Hold

If a client has an overdue second instalment (21+ days), the system automatically moves the engagement to On Hold. You can also manually set On Hold from the engagement detail page — use this if the client requests a pause or has a document issue that blocks progress.

A6 AI Drafts — Review & Approval

The system uses Claude AI to draft client-facing messages — status updates, stage change notifications, and follow-ups. No AI-drafted message reaches a client without a consultant reviewing it first.

Navigate to AI Drafts in the sidebar. Each draft shows:

  • The trigger event (e.g. "Licensing stage advanced — DataFlow submitted")
  • The client it was drafted for
  • The draft message text
  • The channel it will be sent via (email / WhatsApp)
ActionWhat it does
ApproveSends the message as-is to the client via the specified channel
Edit then ApproveOpens the draft for editing before sending — use when the AI draft is mostly right but needs personalisation
RejectDiscards the draft — you should then follow up with the client manually
Mandatory rule: Any draft touching licensing eligibility, visa guidance, or exam-pass likelihood must be reviewed especially carefully. If you are unsure, reject it and send a manually written message. Incorrect guidance in these areas can cause real harm to clients.

A8 Consultant Management & Payouts

Navigate to Consultants in the sidebar. Three tabs: Consultants, Payouts, Applications.

Creating a Staff Consultant

Consultants → New Consultant → fill in name, email, specialisation (UAE / UK / US / Academy), type = STAFF. Staff consultants do not receive commission payouts — their income is handled through payroll.

Reviewing Partner Consultant Applications

Applications tab → open an application → read the bio and qualifications → click Approve or Reject with notes. Approving automatically creates a ConsultantProfile and sets a commission rate (default 75% to consultant, 25% platform fee).

Processing Payouts

Payouts tab shows all pending payouts for partner consultants after a completed consultation. Click Mark Paid once the bank transfer or mobile money transfer has been made. This records the payment date and closes the payout.

★ New — Deactivating a Consultant

When a consultant's engagement with MJN ends but you want to keep their history on file, use Deactivate:

  1. Go to Consultants in the sidebar → Consultants tab.
  2. Find the consultant row and click the amber Deactivate button on the right.
  3. The consultant immediately disappears from the client-facing booking page (/consult). No clients can book them.
  4. Their row in the admin table shows a greyed avatar, strikethrough name, and Deactivated label so you always know who is inactive.
  5. To bring them back at any time, click the green Reactivate button that replaces the Deactivate button. They are immediately live on the booking page again.
Use Deactivate first. It is fully reversible. Only proceed to Remove if you are certain the consultant will never return and you want to clear their profile from the system entirely.

★ New — Removing a Consultant from the System

To permanently remove a consultant when their role is completely done:

  1. Go to Consultants → find the consultant row → click the red trash icon on the far right.
  2. A confirmation modal appears, reminding you that Deactivate is the reversible option.
  3. Click Yes, remove to confirm.
  4. The system will: cancel and clear all future available booking slots for that consultant; set their profile status to INACTIVE; and remove them from the Consultants list in admin.
  5. Past sessions, completed bookings, and payout records are preserved in full for audit purposes — they are not deleted.
This action cannot be undone. The consultant's profile is deactivated at the database level. If they re-join MJN in future they will need to be created as a new consultant profile. Only use Remove when you are certain the engagement is permanently over.

A10 Processing Officers & Escalations

Processing Officers are back-office staff who handle the administrative submission work: sending documents to DataFlow, DHA, NMC, CGFNS, and other regulatory bodies, and tracking those submissions through to completion. They operate within the admin console under their own restricted sidebar.

Creating a Processing Officer Account

Navigate to Staff in the admin sidebar → New Staff Member → set Role to Processing Officer. The account uses email and password login (same as other staff).

Assigning an Officer to an Engagement

Navigate to Officers in the admin sidebar. This page shows:

  • All active processing officers with their current caseload count
  • Active engagements that have not yet been assigned an officer (shown as a warning at the top)

Click Assign Cases next to an officer to open the assignment modal — select which engagements to assign. An officer can be unassigned from a case at any time by selecting "Unassigned" in the same modal.

Best practice: Assign a processing officer at the same time as Stage 4 begins (document collection). Officers should be in place before any regulatory body submission is due.

Consultant Escalation Inbox

When a processing officer escalates a case, the assigned consultant receives a notification and the escalation appears in their Escalations sidebar entry. Consultants can see all open escalations, the reason given by the officer, and can resolve each escalation with a response. Resolved escalations are archived but remain visible for audit purposes.

★ New — Consultant Approvals Queue

Consultants now have a dedicated Approvals page in their sidebar. When a processing officer marks a client update as sensitive, it is held here rather than sent directly. The consultant reviews the draft, then either Approves (delivers it to the client immediately) or Rejects (keeps it as an internal note only). An email notification is sent to the consultant as soon as a sensitive update is queued.

Important: Sensitive updates are not sent to the client until a consultant approves them. Officers should use the sensitive flag for any update involving bad news, delays, application rejections, or anything requiring careful wording.

★ New — Officer Availability & Bulk Reassignment

On the Officers page, each officer card now has an availability toggle. When an officer goes on leave or is otherwise unavailable:

  1. Click the power icon on their card → Mark Unavailable
  2. Optionally select another officer to reassign all their active cases to
  3. Confirm — all affected cases are updated instantly and the replacement officer is notified

To restore availability, click the toggle again → Mark Available. New case assignments can then resume normally.

★ New — Officer Activity Tab on Case Detail

On any case in the Caseload view (/caseload/:id), consultants and admins can now expand an Officer Activity panel. This shows a unified timeline of all officer notes (internal and client-facing), application tracking updates, and escalations for that case — with SLA alerts where applicable.

How to upload

  1. Go to Documents → click Upload Document
  2. Select the document type from the dropdown
  3. Choose your file (PDF or image — max 10MB)
  4. If the document has an expiry date (e.g. passport, licence), enter it

Document expiry alerts

The system scans for expiring documents daily. You will receive reminders at 30 days, 14 days, and 3 days before a document expires. Act immediately — an expired document can halt your licensing application.

B7 Booking a Consultation

  1. Select consultation type: Health, Career, or Both
  2. Choose a consultant from the list (name, specialisation, rating shown)
  3. Select an available date and time slot
  4. Enter your details and any notes for the consultant

Cancelling a booking

Go to Bookings → find your booking → click Cancel. Refund policy: cancellation more than 24 hours before = 100% refund. 4–24 hours before = 50% refund. Less than 4 hours before = no refund.
