Admin Console Guide

For MJN staff, consultants, and operations managers — daily workflow inside admin.mjnhealthcare.com

A1 Logging In & Roles

The admin console is accessed at admin.mjnhealthcare.com. It uses email and password authentication (not phone OTP — that is for candidates only).

admin.mjnhealthcare.com/login

Admin Login

Enter your work email address and password. If you forget your password, contact the system administrator — there is no self-service reset for admin accounts.

RoleAccess levelCan do
Super AdminFull accessEverything — including creating other staff accounts, managing billing, viewing all clients
ConsultantAssigned cases onlyView and manage their own client cases, verify documents, review AI drafts, book sessions
OperationsOperational viewLeads, caseload overview, financial reports — cannot modify engagement letters or payment records

The admin dashboard loads at / after login. It shows a live snapshot of platform activity.

Daily habit: Open the dashboard first thing. Clear the document queue and AI drafts queue before handling other tasks — these directly affect client experience and are time-sensitive.

Advancing a Stage

Open a client's case → scroll to the Milestones section → click Advance Stage once the current stage requirements are confirmed complete. This triggers an automatic client notification and updates the portal dashboard.

Rule: Never advance a stage unless documents for that stage are verified and any associated payment is confirmed. Advancing a stage is a formal record — it cannot be reversed without contacting the system administrator.

Putting a Case On Hold

If a client has an overdue second instalment (21+ days), the system automatically moves the engagement to On Hold. You can also manually set On Hold from the engagement detail page — use this if the client requests a pause or has a document issue that blocks progress.

A5 Document Verification Queue

Navigate to Documents in the sidebar. Documents with status Pending need review.

1
Open the document
Admin / Consultant
Click the document name to view the file. Check: name matches the person record, document is not expired, all fields are legible, translation matches original.
2
Verify or Reject
Admin / Consultant
Click Verify if the document is acceptable. Click Reject and add a clear rejection reason (e.g. "Name mismatch — passport reads ATANGCH but application reads ATANGCH-SIRRIR. Please re-upload the correct page"). The client receives an automatic notification.
3
Client re-uploads
Client
Client sees the rejection reason in their portal and uploads the corrected document. It re-enters the queue as Pending.
Best practice: Always write the rejection reason in plain language the client can understand. Avoid technical jargon. The client may not have a consultant immediately available to explain it.

A6 AI Drafts — Review & Approval

The system uses Claude AI to draft client-facing messages — status updates, stage change notifications, and follow-ups. No AI-drafted message reaches a client without a consultant reviewing it first.

Navigate to AI Drafts in the sidebar. Each draft shows:

  • The trigger event (e.g. "Licensing stage advanced — DataFlow submitted")
  • The client it was drafted for
  • The draft message text
  • The channel it will be sent via (email / WhatsApp)
ActionWhat it does
ApproveSends the message as-is to the client via the specified channel
Edit then ApproveOpens the draft for editing before sending — use when the AI draft is mostly right but needs personalisation
RejectDiscards the draft — you should then follow up with the client manually
Mandatory rule: Any draft touching licensing eligibility, visa guidance, or exam-pass likelihood must be reviewed especially carefully. If you are unsure, reject it and send a manually written message. Incorrect guidance in these areas can cause real harm to clients.

A8 Consultant Management & Payouts

Navigate to Consultants in the sidebar. Three tabs: Consultants, Payouts, Applications.

Creating a Staff Consultant

Consultants → New Consultant → fill in name, email, specialisation (UAE / UK / US / Academy), type = STAFF. Staff consultants do not receive commission payouts — their income is handled through payroll.

Reviewing Partner Consultant Applications

Applications tab → open an application → read the bio and qualifications → click Approve or Reject with notes. Approving automatically creates a ConsultantProfile and sets a commission rate (default 75% to consultant, 25% platform fee).

Processing Payouts

Payouts tab shows all pending payouts for partner consultants after a completed consultation. Click Mark Paid once the bank transfer or mobile money transfer has been made. This records the payment date and closes the payout.

Instalment dunning timeline: Day +7 → payment reminder sent. Day +14 → final warning sent. Day +21 → engagement automatically placed On Hold and admin alerted. To reinstate, collect payment then manually change engagement status back to Active.
Part B

Client Portal Guide

For candidates and healthcare professionals using portal.mjnhealthcare.com

B1 Creating an Account & Logging In

The client portal is at portal.mjnhealthcare.com. Candidates log in using their email address and a one-time password (OTP) sent to their inbox — no password needed.

1
Go to portal.mjnhealthcare.com
Candidate
Open in any browser on your phone or computer. No app download needed.
2
Enter your email address
Candidate
Type the email address you registered with MJN Healthcare. Click Send verification code.
3
Enter the 6-digit OTP
Candidate
Check your inbox for an email from MJN Healthcare. The code arrives within 60 seconds and is valid for 10 minutes. Enter the 6 digits — the boxes auto-advance as you type.
4
Complete your profile (first time only)
Candidate
On first login an onboarding form appears. Fill in: full name, profession (nurse / physician / allied health / student), country, and preferred language (English or French). This cannot be skipped — it is required before accessing any services.
OTP not arriving? Check your spam/junk folder. Wait 60 seconds then tap Resend code. Make sure you are using the same email address provided to MJN — if unsure, contact your consultant.

B2 Dashboard — First Look

After login you land on your dashboard. Here is what each section means:

SectionWhat it showsWhat to do

Documents VerifiedHow many of your uploaded documents have been confirmedUpload missing documents from the Documents page

Tip: The Next Action Banner tells you exactly what to do next. Always start there. If it says "All clear," your case is progressing and no action is needed from you right now.

B3 Case Page — Your Pipeline

Navigate to Case in the sidebar. This is the most detailed view of your engagement.

B4 Documents — Upload & Track

Navigate to Documents in the sidebar. Your consultant will tell you exactly which documents are needed — but the general list for licensing includes:

Document typeTypical requirement
Passport (bio page)Clear scan, all four corners visible, not expired
Nursing / Medical licenceIssued by your home country regulatory body, valid, name must match passport exactly
Academic certificatesDegree/diploma — original or certified copy
TranscriptsOfficial academic record — certified
Experience letter(s)Signed by employer on letterhead, with dates and role
Good Standing CertificateFrom your home country regulatory board, dated within 3 months
Passport-size photoWhite background, recent

How to upload

  1. Go to Documents → click Upload Document
  2. Select the document type from the dropdown
  3. Choose your file (PDF or image — max 10MB)
  4. If the document has an expiry date (e.g. passport, licence), enter it
  5. Click Upload
If a document is rejected: You will receive an SMS and WhatsApp notification with the reason. Return to the Documents page, read the rejection note, and upload the corrected version. Do not upload the same file again without fixing the issue.

Document expiry alerts

The system scans for expiring documents daily. You will receive reminders at 30 days, 14 days, and 3 days before a document expires. Act immediately — an expired document can halt your licensing application.

B5 Checkout — Paying for Services

Navigate to Checkout in the sidebar. Before paying, your engagement letter must be signed.

1
Select your services
Candidate
Services are grouped by category (UAE Licensure, NCLEX, Student Support, etc.). The $50 engagement fee is mandatory and pre-selected. Check the boxes for the services you need — your consultant will have advised you on which ones apply to your pathway.
2
Review your order
Candidate
Step 2 shows your full itemised bill, your billing details, and a "What Happens Next" timeline. The total is shown including tax. If anything is wrong, click Back.
3
Pay via Tranzak
Candidate
Click Proceed to Payment. You are redirected to Tranzak to pay by mobile money (MTN, Orange Money) or card. After payment you are returned to the portal and a PDF receipt is sent to your email and WhatsApp.
Instalments: Some service items (e.g. NCLEX processing) are billed in two instalments. You only pay the first instalment at checkout. The second will be invoiced automatically when the relevant stage of your case is reached. You will receive an SMS and email reminder before the second payment is due.

B6 Academy — Study & Exam Prep

Navigate to Academy in the sidebar. Three tabs: Courses, Study Plan, Practice History.

TabWhat to do
CoursesBrowse available courses. Click Enrol on any course relevant to your exam. Once enrolled, click into the course to access materials and live class schedules.
Study PlanYour personalised weekly study plan. Topics are ordered by priority. Tick a topic as complete when you finish it. Overdue topics are highlighted in amber.
Practice HistoryYour scores from each practice session. Weak areas (topics where you score below 60%) are flagged — these feed your study plan automatically.

Using the AI Study Assistant

A floating chat button appears in the bottom-right of the Academy page. Click it to open the Study Assistant — powered by Claude AI. You can:

  • Ask it to explain any question you got wrong in practice
  • Ask about exam topics (NCLEX pharmacology, DHA anatomy, etc.)
  • Ask for a study strategy for a specific weakness
  • Communicate in English or French — it responds in whichever language you use
Note: The Study Assistant is for exam content and study guidance only. For questions about your case status, visa, or licensing eligibility, contact your assigned consultant directly.

B7 Booking a Consultation

Navigate to Book a Consultation in the sidebar (or go to portal.mjnhealthcare.com/consult).

  1. Select consultation type: Health, Career, or Both
  2. Choose a consultant from the list (name, specialisation, rating shown)
  3. Select an available date and time slot
  4. Enter your details and any notes for the consultant
  5. Pay — you are redirected to Tranzak. On payment confirmation, a Daily.co video room is created and the link is sent to you via email and WhatsApp

You will receive a reminder 24 hours and 1 hour before your session. Click the meeting link in your reminder to join the video call — no app download required.

Cancelling a booking

Go to Bookings → find your booking → click Cancel. Refund policy: cancellation more than 24 hours before = 100% refund. 4–24 hours before = 50% refund. Less than 4 hours before = no refund.

B8 Payments & Receipts

Navigate to Payments in the sidebar to see your full payment history.

  • Each payment shows the date, amount, services paid for, and status
  • Click View Receipt on any paid order to download the PDF receipt
  • Receipts are permanent records — the itemised amounts are locked at the time of payment and never change
  • If you believe a payment has not been recorded, contact your consultant with your Tranzak transaction reference number
Part C

Full Pipeline — Lead to Deployment

Complete end-to-end workflow for a candidate going from first contact to working abroad

This section covers the full-service pathway — licensing + exam prep + job placement. A la carte (individual service) pipelines are in Part D.

C1 Stage 1 — Lead Capture

1
Lead discovers MJN
Lead
Via website, WhatsApp, Facebook, referral, or direct contact. They visit mjnhealthcare.com and interact with the support bot, or message MJN directly on WhatsApp.
2
Contact details captured
System
Website bot captures name, email/phone, profession, and target country. Appears in admin Leads table immediately.
3
Admin assigns to consultant
Admin
Assign within the day. Match consultant to lead based on target country and profession.
4
Consultant contacts within 24 hours
Consultant
WhatsApp preferred. Goal: book the free 30-minute consultation. Send the booking link: mjnhealthcare.com/get-started

C2 Stage 2 — Free Consultation

1
Free 30-minute consultation call
Consultant + Candidate
Via Daily.co video or phone. Consultant reviews: profession, qualifications, current registration status, target country, timeline expectations, and financial readiness.
2
Pathway mapped
Consultant
Consultant determines the exact licensing body, exam requirements, and document list. Gives a realistic timeline and total cost estimate. Be honest — overpromising timelines or pass rates is not permitted.
3
Decision — proceed or not
Candidate
Candidate decides to proceed. If yes, consultant creates the Person record in the system (if not already existing) and initiates the Engagement.

C3 Stage 3 — Engagement & Payment

1
Engagement letter sent
System (Dropbox Sign)
Admin triggers the engagement letter from the admin console. Candidate receives it via email. The letter details: exact services included, total fees, payment schedule, and explicit disclaimers (no guarantee of exam pass, visa approval, or job placement).
2
Candidate signs the letter
Candidate
Signed electronically via Dropbox Sign. The portal Case page shows Signed status once complete. No work proceeds and no payment is taken before the letter is signed.
3
Candidate completes checkout
Candidate
Goes to portal Checkout. Selects services as advised by consultant. $50 engagement fee is mandatory. First instalment of any 2-part fee is charged. Payment via Tranzak (mobile money or card).
4
Receipt issued, consultant notified
System
PDF receipt sent to candidate via email and WhatsApp. Consultant receives a notification that payment is confirmed and work can begin.

C4 Stage 4 — Document Collection

1
Consultant sends document checklist
Consultant
Via WhatsApp or email. The checklist is specific to the candidate's pathway and regulatory body. For UAE DataFlow: passport, licence, certificates, transcripts, experience letters, good standing, photo.
2
Candidate uploads documents to portal
Candidate
One by one in the Documents section. Each upload is stored securely in Cloudflare R2 (encrypted).
3
Admin verifies each document
Admin / Consultant
Checks name consistency, validity, and completeness. Approves or rejects with reason. Rejected documents are flagged to the candidate for re-upload.
4
All documents verified
Consultant
Once all required documents are verified, consultant advances the stage to "Documents Complete" and proceeds to submit to the regulatory body.

C5 Stage 5 — Licensing Application

1
POA / Letter of Authorization collected
Consultant + Candidate
Before submitting anything to a third party on the candidate's behalf, a signed Power of Attorney or Letter of Authorization is required. This is separate from the engagement letter.
2
Submission to regulatory body
Consultant
E.g. DataFlow submission for UAE, CGFNS for US NCLEX, NMC for UK. Consultant submits on behalf of the candidate, tracks the reference number, and advances the portal stage.
3
Monitoring and follow-up
Consultant
DataFlow takes 45–90 days. Consultant monitors regularly and follows up with the regulatory body if processing is delayed. Status updates sent to candidate every 2 weeks minimum.
4
Application approved
Regulatory body → Consultant → System
Consultant marks stage complete. Candidate notified. Second instalment (if applicable) is now invoiced automatically by the system.

C6 Stage 6 — Exam Preparation & Sitting

1
Enrol in Academy exam prep course
Candidate
Via portal Academy tab. Consultant advises which course matches the exam (DHA, NCLEX, HAAD, NMC CBT, etc.).
2
Follow study plan and attend live classes
Candidate
Study plan adapts weekly based on practice scores. AI Study Assistant available for question explanations. Attend live sessions on scheduled dates.
3
Exam booked
Consultant + Candidate
Consultant assists with exam scheduling (Prometric for DHA, Pearson VUE for NCLEX). ATT/exam authorisation codes have expiry dates — book promptly.
4
Candidate sits exam and receives result
Candidate
Results are typically available within 48 hours (DHA) or on the day (NCLEX via quick results). Candidate shares result with consultant. Stage advanced accordingly.
Failed exam: Consultant books a follow-up call to review weak areas and update the study plan. A retake can be arranged — fees depend on the regulatory body's retake policy. Retake service items may apply.

C7 Stage 7 — Job Placement

1
CV preparation
Consultant
Consultant reviews and prepares the candidate's CV for the target market. UAE employers expect a specific format different from Cameroonian/West African CVs.
2
Submitted to partner employers
Consultant
MJN's verified employer partners (hospitals, clinics, agencies) are matched to the candidate's profile and licence. CVs submitted on behalf of the candidate.
3
Interview preparation
Consultant + Candidate
Consultant briefs candidate on employer expectations, common interview questions for the target country, and salary negotiation norms.
4
Offer received and accepted
Consultant + Candidate
Consultant reviews the offer letter with the candidate. Checks: salary, benefits, visa sponsorship, start date, contract duration. Advises on negotiation if needed.

C8 Stage 8 — Deployment & Follow-up

1
Visa and relocation support
Consultant
Employer typically sponsors the work visa. Consultant advises on what documents to prepare for the visa application and what to expect on arrival.
2
Candidate arrives and starts work
Candidate
MJN remains the point of contact for the first 90 days in-country. Any issues with the employer or local registration are escalated through the consultant.
3
Engagement marked Complete
Consultant
Admin marks the engagement as Complete in the system. Partner commission invoice is triggered automatically if applicable. Candidate's success story is recorded.
4
CPD and ongoing relationship
Consultant + Candidate
Candidate remains in the MJN community. CPD programs available for licence renewal. Document expiry reminders continue automatically for passport, licence, and certifications.
Part D

A La Carte Services

Individual service pathways for candidates who need a specific service, not the full pipeline

All a la carte services still require a signed engagement letter and the $50 engagement fee. The difference is that services outside the selected scope are not included and not billed.

For candidates who are already employed or have an employer, and only need the licensing process managed.

Pricing note: DataFlow verification fee varies by profession — nurse vs physician. The correct fee is auto-selected at checkout based on the profession on the candidate's profile.

D2 NCLEX Exam Prep Only

For candidates who have already begun the NCLEX process independently and need structured exam support only — or those targeting the US from scratch.

StepWhoDetail

D3 Student Support Only

For healthcare students seeking internship placement or university application guidance.

D4 Academy / Exam Prep Only

For professionals who only need structured study support and are managing their licensing independently.

  • Candidate creates a portal account and enrols in the relevant exam prep course
  • Access to question banks (5,000+ questions, updated to current exam blueprints)
  • AI Study Assistant for question rationale and personalised guidance
  • Live virtual classes (Daily.co) — small cohorts, real instructors
  • Study plan auto-generated from diagnostic and practice performance
  • Weak-area detection flags topics for extra review
  • Available in English and French
Academy-only access does not include document verification, licensing submission, or job placement. Candidates can upgrade to a full engagement at any point by signing an engagement letter and paying the engagement fee.
